Bollinger Bands define whether price is high or low on a relative basis.

By definition: the prices are high at the Upper Bollinger Band and low at the Lower Bollinger Band.

The moment price breaks out the bands expand dramatically and they keep the definition of `Relatively High` and `Relatively Low` germane to the price regime.

Inside the deviation, the deviation from the average are squared in.
If you square a small deviation, a number too small, its small.
But if you square a bige devivation, a big number, we get a big number. And that big number drives the band wider thereby keeping our definition of `Relatively High` and `Relatively Low` germane to the price regime.
