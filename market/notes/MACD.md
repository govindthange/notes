`Question:` How do you know when to enter the trade? What if the price just move against your posisition as soon you enter the trade?

`Answer:` Use MACD indicator to identifying the short term momentum using the crossover betweeen MACD line and Signal lines.

> MACD is best suited in a trending market. MACD are more effective when used with a long term trend indicator like 200 SMA or 100 EMA.

> MACD can also be used to confirm entries near the Resistance & Support levels.


`MACD is a leading indicator`

MACD not only utilizes past data to calculate its signals but it also helps in predicting the possible change in the given direction before other indicators and hence leads in its signal. It successfully predicts a potential reversal by showing a gradual increase/decrease in the strength of the momentum via its histogram bar size and fading colors.

# Moving Average Convergence Divergence

MACD helps in seeing where the buy-sell money flow changes. This is indicated by the crossovers. ^9e1687

MACD is a trend following indicator and the momentum indicator. It has 4 components:

## 1. MACD Line (Blue)

MACD line is very sensitive to the price changes and moves much faster.

`MACD Line` = `12 Day EMA` - `26 Day EMA`

## 2. Signal Line (Organge/Red)

Signal line reacts slower to the price changes.

`Signal Line` = 9 Day EMA of `MACD Line`

## 3. MACD Crossovers and Histogram

![[#^9e1687]]

Histogram measures the short term momentum in the market. When the histogram bars becomes larger and larger then it tells you that there is a strong momentum building up.

MACD histogram is the difference between the MACD Line and the Signal Line.

`MACD Histogram` = `MACD Line` - `Signal Line`

- If the blue line crossesover the organge line upwards then the histogram shows green bars above the Zero Line.

- If the blue line crossesover the orange line downards then the histogram shows red bars below the Zero Line.

The size of the histogram bars indicates the strength of the momentum.

- The gap between the MACD Line and Singal Line affects the size of the histogram bars; the larger the gap, the bigger the histogram will be.
	- As the 2 lines goes further apart, i.e thir gap increases, the histogram bars gets bigger.
	- As the 2 lines comes closer, i.e., their gap tightens, the histogram bars becomes smaller.

> Note that MACD histogram's strength is different from the relative strength depicted by the [[RSI]]. RSI shows relative strength of the gains against the losses. It shows the size of the Green candles vs the Red candles.

## 4. Zero Line

Center of the MACD chart

- In an uptrend (`price over 200 EMA`) if the `MACD Line crosses above the Signal Line` and the `crossover is below the Zero Line` it indicates bounce after a deeper pullback.

- In a downtrend (`price below 200 EMA`) if the `MACD Line crosses below the Signal Line` and the `crossover is above the Zero Line` it indicates bounce after a deeper pullback.

# Predicting Reversals & Breakouts around key levels

Whenever price comes close to the key levels (like Support, Resistance or Trendlines), you don't directly go Long/Short. You must analyze how the price is approaching those key areas.

If the price move is showing `momentum` i.e. it approaches the key levels with strong & clean moves (i.e. big candles) then there is a high probability of a reversal.

If the price move is showing `a lacks of moementum` i.e. it approaches the key levels slowly with choppy moves (smaller candles or candles with long wicks) then there is a high probability of seeing a breakout.
- If the price approaches resistance by making higher highs, higher lows and some side ways movements then it is highly likely that it will break through the resistance.
- If the price slowly approaches the support by making lower highs, lower lows with lot of side ways movement then it is highly likely that it will break through the support.

`So how do you know whether the momentum is strong or not?`
You look at the size of the histogram bar size.

## Momentum Reversals

### Identifying Market Turning Points

1. A move into the [[Market Structure]] like support, resistance or a trendline.
2. MACD histogram shows momentum
3. Price rejection at the S&R level or trendline. Take confluence from candlestick reversal patterns (hammer, shooting star etc).

Say the price is approaching a resistance level and MACD histogram shows bigger bars while candlesticks shows bearish reversal pattern then it indicates a strong likelyhood of a reversal taking place.

You must **take confluence from chart patterns or candliestick reversal patterns** before taking a position. There are no guarantees in the market! MACD will just aid in confirmation.

### Do not chase the breakouts here!

Whenever you see `a strong momentum into a market structure`, i.e. a key level like support, resistance or trendline, you should **never fomo in** and open a new posistion in the direction of the price movement.

However counter intuitive it may sound, DO NOT DO THIS! There is always a higher probability of a reversal than a breakout.


## Trend Filtering

Use MACD to filter trend and have a bias so that you are always by default trading on the right side of the trend.

1. If a higher timeframe `MACD Line` crosses above `Signal Line` then have a `Long Bias`
2. If a higher timeframe `MACD Line` crosses below `Signal Line` then have a `Short Bias`

## High Probability Breakouts

1. Look for a build up in the [[Market Structure]] around support, resistance, or trendlines.
2. MACD histogram shows a build up with low to no momentum. The histogram is almost flat along the Zero Line.

With shrinking histogram bars indicates sign of breakout however note that predicting a direction is anyone's guess. Its difficult to point out a specific direction. Whats clear though is that a big move is eminent.
