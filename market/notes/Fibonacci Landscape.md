# Plot the fibonacci landscape

## Step 1. Plot the 1st horizontal channel
[...](https://youtu.be/mu91D_4Vd5c)

- Plot the first 2 points of the channel by connecting the 2 market cycle peaks (2 swing highs).
- Plot the 3rd point of the channel by dragging the channel down to the swing low of the market cycle bottom (swing low).

## Step 2. Plot the 2nd horizontal channel

Extend the 1st horizontal channel on the top side.
- Select the 0.3 on the first channel and drag it to 0.6.

## Step 3. Plot the 1st vertical channel
[...](https://youtu.be/mu91D_4Vd5c?t=819)

- Plot the first 2 points of the channel by connecting the first market cycle peak (1st swing high) to the bottom of the market cycle (swing low).
- Plot the 3rd point of the channel by dragging the channel to the left to connect to the 2nd market cycle (2nd swing high).

## Step 4. Plot the 2nd vertical channel

Extend the 1st vertical channel on the left side.
	- Select the 0.618 on the first channel to 0.618

## Step 5. Plot the 3rd vertical channel

Extend the 1st vertical channel on the right side.

## Step 6. Plot the 1st fibonacci circle

Connect the 2 market cycle peaks (the 2 swing highs).
[...](https://youtu.be/mu91D_4Vd5c?t=1007)

## Step 7. Plot the 2nd fibonacci cirlce

Connect the 2nd market cycle peak (swing high) to the 2nd market cycle bottom (swing low).
