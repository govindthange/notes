It tells how fast the premium will change.

- Overall try to keep vega neutral.
	- On budget days you may use Theta/Vega ratio of 1:1.
- If volatility rises then try to keep vega slightly positive.
	- Vega becomes positive when you take your hedges into calendar (next expiry).
	- The farther the expiry is, more positive the vega will be.
	- Mostly vega is positive in debit strategy.
- If volatility falls then you can have negative vega.
	- Mostly vega is negative in credit strategy.
	- You are negative vega when you end up paying more in hedges.
	- You are negative vega when you are more long than short.
- Going long on vega means...
	- It means keeping vega positive.
	- It means longing volatility.
	- It means you expect volatility to go up.
	- It means it will support trending market.
- Going short on vega means...
	- It means keeping vega negative.
	- It means shorting volatility.
	- It means you expect volatility to go down.
	- It means it will support sideways market.
