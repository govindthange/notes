# Vertical Credit Spread - Tuesday 3 PM Deployment
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A8ucaccWoUg)

1. Ensure `VIX between 18-30`.
2. Form a bullish/bearish `bias from chart` using technical analysis.
3. Form a bullish/bearish `bias from OI change`.
	- Reliable OI data is not ready by Thursday, Friday, or Monday.
	- Reliable OI data is available is available only on Tuesday. You can use this to understand where are boundaries formed.
	- If you deploy it on Tuesday, you get good theta decay on Tuesday and Wednesday.
	- Identify where is the maximum OI change.
	- Check whether call writing OI change (resistance) is higher or put writing OI change (support).
		- If OI change on call writing side is high then its bearish.
		- If OI change on put writing side is high then its bullish.
4. Form a bullish/bearish `bias from max pain`.
	1. If the bars on the call side is bigger than put side, then its bearish.
	2. If the bars on the put side is bigger than call side, then its bullish.
5. Once your bias has a `confluence from charts, OI change, and max pain` charts then proceed with trade.
6. Now create a vertical spread to support your bias.
