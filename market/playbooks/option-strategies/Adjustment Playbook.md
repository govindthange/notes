# Non Directional Strategy

## Protecting the call side

###  1. Shift put side upwards
[...](https://youtu.be/_2f5mNtDVfk?t=1132)

- Rebalance delta/premium by sliding the put side leg upwards.
- By sliding the put side leg it slightly shifts/extends its B/E outwards on the call side.
