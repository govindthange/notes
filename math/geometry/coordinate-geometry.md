# 📐 Coordinate Geometry Formulas

## I. Point and Line Basics

* Distance Formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$

* Section Formula (Internal): $\left(\frac{mx_2 + nx_1}{m + n}, \frac{my_2 + ny_1}{m + n}\right)$

* Section Formula (External): $\left(\frac{mx_2 - nx_1}{m - n}, \frac{my_2 - ny_1}{m - n}\right)$

* Centroid of a Triangle: $\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2 + x_3}{3}, \frac{y_1 + y_2 + y_3}{3}\right)$

* Incenter of a Triangle: $\left(\frac{ax_1 + bx_2 + cx_3}{a + b + c}, \frac{ay_1 + by_2 + cy_3}{a + b + c}\right)$

* Area of a Triangle: $\Delta = \frac{1}{2} |x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)|$

* Angle Between Two Lines: $\tan \theta = \left| \frac{m_1 - m_2}{1 + m_1 m_2} \right|$

* Distance of a Point from a Line: $d = \frac{|Ax_1 + By_1 + C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$

* Distance Between Parallel Lines: $d = \frac{|C_1 - C_2|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$

## II. Pair of Straight Lines

* Homogeneous Equation of Second Degree: $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 = 0$

* Angle Between Pair of Homogeneous Lines: $\tan \theta = \frac{2\sqrt{h^2 - ab}}{a + b}$

* General Equation of Second Degree: $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$

* Condition for General Equation to Represent Lines: $abc + 2fgh - af^2 - bg^2 - ch^2 = 0$

## III. Circles

* Standard Equation of a Circle: $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$

* General Equation of a Circle: $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$ (Center: $(-g, -f)$, Radius: $\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$)

* Equation of Tangent (Point Form): $xx_1 + yy_1 + g(x + x_1) + f(y + y_1) + c = 0$

* Equation of Tangent (Slope Form): $y = mx \pm r\sqrt{1 + m^2}$

* Length of Tangent from a Point: $L = \sqrt{x_1^2 + y_1^2 + 2gx_1 + 2fy_1 + c}$

* Condition for Orthogonality of Two Circles: $2g_1 g_2 + 2f_1 f_2 = c_1 + c_2$

## IV. Parabola

* Standard Equations: $y^2 = 4ax$ (Right), $y^2 = -4ax$ (Left), $x^2 = 4ay$ (Up), $x^2 = -4ay$ (Down)

* Parametric Coordinates ($y^2 = 4ax$): $(at^2, 2at)$

* Equation of Tangent ($y^2 = 4ax$, Point Form): $yy_1 = 2a(x + x_1)$

* Equation of Tangent ($y^2 = 4ax$, Slope Form): $y = mx + \frac{a}{m}$

* Equation of Normal ($y^2 = 4ax$, Slope Form): $y = mx - 2am - am^3$

* Length of Latus Rectum: $4a$

## V. Ellipse

* Standard Equation ($\text{Major Axis on } x$-axis): $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$ (where $a > b$)

* Eccentricity ($e$): $e = \sqrt{1 - \frac{b^2}{a^2}}$

* Foci Coordinates: $(\pm ae, 0)$

* Directrices: $x = \pm \frac{a}{e}$

* Length of Latus Rectum: $\frac{2b^2}{a}$

* Equation of Tangent (Slope Form): $y = mx \pm \sqrt{a^2 m^2 + b^2}$

## VI. Hyperbola

* Standard Equation: $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$

* Eccentricity ($e$): $e = \sqrt{1 + \frac{b^2}{a^2}}$

* Foci Coordinates: $(\pm ae, 0)$

* Asymptotes: $\frac{x}{a} \pm \frac{y}{b} = 0$

* Rectangular Hyperbola: $xy = c^2$

## VII. Three-Dimensional Coordinate Geometry

* Distance Formula in 3D: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}$

* Direction Cosines ($l, m, n$): $l^2 + m^2 + n^2 = 1$

* Angle Between Two Lines with Direction Ratios $(a_1, b_1, c_1)$ and $(a_2, b_2, c_2)$: $\cos \theta = \frac{a_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2 + c_1 c_2}{\sqrt{a_1^2 + b_1^2 + c_1^2}\sqrt{a_2^2 + b_2^2 + c_2^2}}$

* Equation of a Plane: $Ax + By + Cz + D = 0$

* Distance of a Point from a Plane: $d = \frac{|Ax_1 + By_1 + Cz_1 + D|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + C^2}}$
