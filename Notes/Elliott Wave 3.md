# Rules
- w3 cannot be the shortest of w1 and w5

# Conventions
- Commonly any 2 of the 3 impulse waves (w1, w3 and w5) will be approximately equal.
- If w3 < 1.618 of fibo(w1, reverse=true) then w3 is a normal wave. Here either w1 or w5 will be an extended wave.
- If w3 > 1.618 of fibo(w1, reverse=true) then w3 will undergo extension.
- Generally w3 extends.
- Typically w3 is steeper than w1 as price moves suddenly and swiftly in w3. It looks almost vertical on the chart and therefore very easy to spot.
- Volume spikes higher during the 3rd wave.
- One will frequently see gaps in prices during w3 as stop losses are hit and more and more people wake up to the fact that price is running away from them.
- Do not rush to take profits while w3 is undergoing extension.

# Tips
- Stop Loss whould be a few pips below the start of w1. Note that by rule, w2 wll not extend/retrace below the start of w1. If w2 exceeds below the start of w1 then the Elliott Wave Strategy has failed and you will abort the trade.
- Enter the trade when w2 retraces to 0.5 or 0.618 level of fibo (w1, reverse=false)
- While w3 is undergoing extension (i.e., it crosses 1.618 level) **DO NOT** rush to take profits. Its normal for w3 to extend tremendously.

# Tracking

As w3 grows beyond the size of w1 then the next target level is at 1.236 of fibo(w1, reverse=true)

After crossing 1.236 level the subsequent taret level is at 1.618 of fibo(w1, reverse=true)

After crossing 1.618 level w3 would undergo `Extension`

Once w3 extends it will briefly pause at following fibonacci levels:
- 2
- 2.618
- 3
- 4.25
