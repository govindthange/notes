- In smaller size trading accounts knowing and managing deltas is an essential aspect of an overall trade strategy.
- Knowing and managing the delta of your portfolio regardless of the size of your account is an essential risk management tool and a key to successful trading.

# Step 1. Know your deltas

- To find out the overall delta use the beta weight function on your trading platform. Generally, in a small account weigh it against the index.
- Unweighted deltas dont help. You want to compare apples to apples.

# Step 2. Adjust your deltas

Once we know our delta, there are many ways we can adjust it higher or lower.

1. One common way is to sell an OTM put or OTM call credit spread.
	- Sell a put against a short delta position.
	- Sell a call against a long delta position.

| Short Too Many Deltas | Long Too Many Deltas |
| --------------------- | -------------------- |
| Sell OTM put spread   | Sell OTM call spread |

2. Another way to adjust the deltas of your porfolio is through a contrariran play. By either selling a call or a put on an individual stock that has moved in the direction of the deltas you need to balance.
