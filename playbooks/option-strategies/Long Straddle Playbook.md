# Step 1. Wait for the setup

Wait for the mouse trap pattern.

1. Look for a candlestick pattern on a `daily chart` where the market is trapped in a very `tight range` for `3 consecutive days`.
2. Suppose for last 2 days, nifty is trapped in a tight range.
3. Now on the 2rd day, we wait till afternoon 3:20 PM when pmarket is about to close.
4. If we see the 3rd day too is about to end in the same range then we are ready with our breakout setup.

# Step 2. Buy ATM straddle

Buy ATM PE/CE of current month/week @ 3:20 PM on the 3rd day.

- Do not initiate position on the 4th day.
- Do not select a contract with 3 DTE. In this case, select the next expiry.

# Step 3. Exit

- Typically profit will show up on the immediate next day or within 2 days.
- Exit this position within 3 days of deployment. Book the loss due to time decays.
