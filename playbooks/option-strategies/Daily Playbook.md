# EoD Short - Strangle / Iron Condor
[...](https://youtu.be/5cu3PTHSDOM?t=459)

## Step 1. Wait for EoD

1. Wait for the last hour of trading (2 PM central time)

## Step 2. Enter position

Do 1σ on the short
- EITHER short strangle for smaller underlyings.
- OR short iron condors for larger underlyings.

## Step 3. Wait for BoD

Let the bots and algorithm to clear up.

## Step 3. Exit position

- About 5 min. after the market opens, start taking those trades off (9:40 AM).
- Over the next 10 or 15 min close out all the trades open you opened on the previous session's last trading hours.

# Intraday Short Straddle - Jegan | CapitalZonex
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FDaeAqwqjgg)

1. Sell ATM CE and ATM PE @ 1 PM
	- You may enter at 12:30 PM or 12:45 PM too.
	- Strike price is based on spot price @ 1 PM.
	- Each leg will have fixed SL from 20-50%
	- Never have SL based on combined premium.
2. One SL is hit, leave the other SL open.
3. Trail your SL once SL is hit.
4. Exit position @ 3:15 PM
