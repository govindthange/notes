# Step 3. Define risk

Hedge naked strangle using Iron Condor.

1. Define risk with 2:5 (2 buy: 5 short).
	- You can even try 1:3.
3. Use strikes around ATM for hedging with a ratio.
	- Use the received credit to buy put & call of the same expiry.
	- Adjust the strikes around ATM to neutralize delta.
	- It is fine if the strategy becomes slightly debit.
