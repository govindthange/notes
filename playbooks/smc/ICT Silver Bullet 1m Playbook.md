> This strategy does not require any bullish/bearish bias. It only requires you to spot a liquidity sweep to the down side or up side.

# Step 1. Wait for a liquidity sweep
[...](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=75) | [example](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=476)

1. Switch to 1m timeframe.
2. Look for a buy side or sell side liquidity sweep.

# Step 2. Wait for  MSS w/ displacement
[...](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=103) | [example](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=483)

Displacement occurs when the market moves with lot of volume and large candles.

1. Wait for the market to reverse.
2. Wait for the 1st Market Structure Shift (MSS) with displacement (FVG).
3. Wait for a FVG to fully form on the displacement leg. Ignore other FVGs before it.
4. Finally mark the 50% of the FVG.
5. Wait for the market to fill this 50% zone.

# Step 3. Enter off FVG/BPR
[...](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=162) | [example](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=608)

A balanced price range (BPR) is the double FVG. BPR is created when the 2 opposite FVG overlaps. It is an added confluence to enter a high probability trade.

Again, you need not wait for BPR. Entering at the 50% of the FVG is equally valid.

Place a limit order @ 50% of FVG.
# Step 4. Place SL at the nearest swing high/low
[...](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=215)

1. Risk no more than 1% per trade.
2. Place SL at th enearest swing high/low with 1 spread gap.

# Step 5. Place target at the next swing high/low or at 1:2 RR
[...](https://youtu.be/iQ63gceKbZg?t=238)

We look for anything more than 1:2 RRR.

- 10 handles to next draw on liquidity (Indices)
- 15 pips to next draw on liquidity (Forex).

The above numbers are certainly not the max!
