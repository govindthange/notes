# Step 1.  Mark market structure in HTF

1. Follow [[SMC Playbook]] steps.
2. Follow [[SMC MTF Entry Playbook]] steps.
3. Analyze market structure in HTF (preferably 15 min)
4. Mark an order block in the HTF.

# Step 2. Wait for price to tap OB in HTF

![[smc-HTF-15m-OB-tap-EURUSD.png]]

![[smc-HTF-15m-OB-tap-USDJPY.png]]
# Step 3. Switch to LTF
[...](https://youtu.be/s1TCUQs6HPA?t=472)

Switch to LTF (if HTF is 15m then use 1m as LTF).

# Step 4. Wait for CHoCH in LTF

Wait for price to form a CHoCH in LTF and close beyond it.

![[smc-LTF-entry-EURUSD-1m.png]]

![[smc-LTF-entry-EURJPY-1m.png]]

# Step 5. Take RSI confirmation in LTF

Take RSI confirmation upon CHoCH.

Analyze RSI in LTF (preferably 1 min) and wait for the divergence signals for confirmation.

![[smc-LTF-EURUSD-entry-rsi-confirmation.png]]

![[smc-LTF-USDJPY-entry-rsi-confirmation.png]]


# Step 6. Mark OB in LTF

Upon CHoCH and RSI confirmation mark the minor order block (mOB) in LTF.

![[smc-LTF-entry-EURUSD-1m-OB.png]]

![[smc-LTF-entry-EURJPY-1m-OB.png]]

# Step 7. Wait for price to approach OB in LTF

1. Wait for price to approach OB in LTF.
2. Be ready to place a limit order at this LTF minor Order Block.

# Step 8. Enter position in LTF

Place a limit order before the price taps this LTF Order Block. Use 1:2 R/R. You may set next major level structure as target.

![[smc-LTF-EURUSD-entry-1st-take-profit.png]]

![[smc-LTF-USDJPY-entry-2nd-take-profit.png]]

# Step 9. Exit position

> Never terminate your trade before price reaches your targets.

#### Target 1. 1:2 R/R level

Exit 50% of the total position when the price reaches 1:2 R/R level. With this you will be at break-even even if the price hits your stop loss.

#### Target 2. The next major level structure

For the second take profit, target the next major level structure in front of the market in HTF (15m).

![[smc-LTF-EURUSD-entry-2nd-take-profit.png]]

![[smc-LTF-USDJPY-entry-2nd-take-profit.png]]

# Step 10. Trail stop loss in LTF

1. Keep track of your profits.
2. Every time the price makes a new higher low in LTF, move your SL couple of pips below the lowest point of the higher low. Follow the opposite for bearish trade.
	![[smc-LTF-EURUSD-EURUSD-entry-sl-trail1.png]]
3. Keep trailing this SL as new higher lows are formed.
	![[smc-LTF-EURUSD-entry-sl-trail2.png]]

# Step 11. Ride trend in LTF (Optional)

> This is an optional step and should be practiced after gaining experience.

As and when the price forms new order blocks in LTF, place a new low risk position with a lot size 50% of that used for the first CHoCH based entry.

![[smc-LTF-multiple-EURUSD-entries-OB2.png]]

The price may not tap all these order blocks created by BoS, so you may miss few trades.

![[smc-LTF-multiple-EURUSD-entries-OB3.png]]

The price may hit some SLs and you may lose modest amount of your capital, which is fine. If you see below, SL was hit for 2nd OB trade.

![[smc-LTF-multiple-USDJPY-entries-OB4.png]]

When creating subsequent BoS trades do not allocate more than 50% of the lot size which you first risked for CHoCH Order Block trade.

![[smc-LTF-multiple-EURUSD-entries-OB3-buy.png]]

![[smc-LTF-multiple-USDJPY-entries-OB4-sell.png]]

Never have more than 3 trades open for the same analysis.

![[smc-LTF-multiple-EURUSD-3entries.png]]

#### RSI Bullish/Bearish Divergence Signal

RSI divergences indicates the potential divergence possibility in the market. An RSI divergence occurs when price moves in the opposite direction of the RSI.

![[smc-rsi-divergences.png]]

RSI Bearish Divergence: occurs when the RSI creates an overbought reading followed by a lower high that appears with higher highs on the price. See yellow lines in above image.

RSI Bullish Divergence: occurs when the RSI displays an oversold reading followed by a higher low that appears with lower lows in the price. See red lines in above image.
