
# 1. Standard Continuation Entry
[...](https://youtu.be/vtZ6hWYT_nM?t=800)

- Every time the market creates an OB we can place a trade if the direction is on our side in both HTF and LTF.
- Market presents a lot of entries in this pattern so risk management is important.

# 2. Supply/Demand Flip Entry
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtZ6hWYT_nM&t=620s)

- This pattern is most effective when the price aggressively pushes  away from demand/supply zone, and rapidly breaks through the "last demand zone" leaving inefficiency behind.

![[smc-htf-entry-supply-demand-flip.png]]

![[smc-htf-entry-supply-demand-flip-example.png]]

# 3. LTF CHoCH Entry
[...](https://youtu.be/vtZ6hWYT_nM?t=697)

- This pattern occurs after mitigating HTF supply/demand.
- Trade this pattern when market is in our direction in HTF.
- Essentially when market taps into a HTF order block, you switch to LTF and enter upon CHoCH in LTF.
- CHoCH trades are most effective
	- When they break through 2 or more supply/demand zones.
	- CHoCH forms after a HTF mitigation (as mentioned in above points)
	- Price impulsively breaks through the zones, with few large candles
- Follow [[SMC Entry Playbook]]

![[smc-htf-entry-ltf-choch.png]]
