
Do the top down analysis.

> Analyzing the bigger picture helps you to enhance your ability to take more precise and confident entries.

Whenever you are confused about the direction, you are focusing on the wrong timeframe. If you directly trade in a LTF (say 5m/1m), the market would look choppy without any visible direction. The price often continuously violates the structure levels to both up and down sides.

When you zoom out to HTF (say 1h), then you can quickly identify market trend. By knowing HTF directional bias we can take entries with confidence in LTF.

# Step 1. Mark key levels in HTF
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dnnFb9V9uLI&t=360s)

Use weekly and daily time frames to only mark the key levels that price has recently reacted to.

1. Switch to weekly time frame.
2. On the weekly time frame only draw the most recent levels near the current price that have high chances for the price to react to when it taps into them. [AUS/US Weekly Chart](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=832)
3. Switch to daily time frame.
4. On the daily time frame, adjust the weekly levels to get the greatest number of touches from the daily perspective. [AUS/US Daily Chart](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=842)
5. Draw additional daily key levels of the market structure with another color.

We use weekly/daily levels in following ways:
1. `To identify market direction`: Breaking each one into the up/downsides indicates whether the supply or demand is in control and price can continue pushing to the next level.
2. `To set our targets`

## Marking guidelines

- Higher the time frame, the greater the importance of its levels, so draw daily and weekly levels with different colors.
- Draw the levels where you could get the greatest number of touches.
- Drawing from the bodies of the candles has a higher priority.
- Treat levels as areas, not solid lines.

### Marking criteria
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=448)

The key level should satisfy following criteria

#### Criteria 1. The turning points
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=457)

- Turning points are the areas that have reversed the market trend previously.
- Market sees these areas as overvalued/oversold and there is a higher chance for the price to react to these levels when it reaches them again.

#### Criteria 2. Multiple rejections
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dnnFb9V9uLI&t=473s)

- Multiple rejections from an area shows that the traders took action at the same level at different times making it more powerful.
- More rejections are better but it won't guarantee that the level will hold.
- When the market taps into a level we closely watch price action to indicate what is happening. If the rejection is getting weaker every time there is a higher chance for the price to break through this level but it will be strong support if the price reaches this area again.

#### Criteria 3. Acted as both support & resistance
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=503)

If a level of market structure has acted as both support and resistance previously it increases the probability for the price to reject this level again

#### Criteria 4. The move away from the area was drastic
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=516)

This is a level where the price did not show a tiny reaction but a true reversal. The deeper the return from a level the more important that level is.

#### Criteria 5. Recently respected or created
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dnnFb9V9uLI&t=529s)

The recent most levels in front of the price are always more effective since they are current and new whether they are traditional support, resistance, or an order block.

# Step 3. Analyze market features in MTF
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=543)

Analyze charts after marking SMC and ICT structures in MTF.

Again, both weekly and daily levels must be clearly visible on MTF (4h/1h). Each of these levels will act as a strong support/resistance when the market reaches them.

1. Switch to 4h. [AUS/USD 4h Chart](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=852)
2. Apply SMC on 4h time frame. This includes identifying the following. [...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=577)
	1. Market direction
	2. Supply/Demand areas
	3. Order blocks
	4. Fair value gaps
	5. Liquidity zones
	6. Trading opportunity
3. 4h time frame for AUS/USD example presents us with 2 scenarios [...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=856)
	1. If LTF price action shows bearish signals at this order block, then we could go short and our primary target will be the daily level in front of the price. Our secondary target will be the weekly level.
	2. On the contrary, if the price manages to close above the order block then our 4h directional bias will change to bullish and we expect the price to reach the daily and weekly levels above the price.
4. Switch to 1h time frame to observe more details. [AUS/USD 1h Chart](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=886)
5. Apply SMC on the 1h time frame. [...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=634)
6. You want 4h and 1h to be in the same direction.

# Step 4. Trade in LTF
[...](https://youtu.be/dnnFb9V9uLI?t=691)

The LTF (15m/5m) is the last time for analysis in the process.

Use 15m/5m for taking confirmations and entering trades.
- For 4h time frame order block (OB), we look for confirmation in 15m.
- For 1h time frame order block (OB), we look for confirmation in 5m.

Follow [[SMC Entry Playbook]] to take entry like so:
1. Wait for the price to tap the OB in middle time frame.
2. Switch to LTF.
3. Wait for CHoCH (in LTF) to confirm that the short term pullback/throwback move is over and market may continue in the direction of the trend.
4. Set the daily levels in front of the price as the first target.
5. Once the daily level is rached, the price may continue pushing to reach the next weekly level in front of the price.
