# Exponential Moving Averages
~ by Govind Thange (Based on BTC Charts Analysis)


`50, 100, 200, & 400 Daily EMAs:` Good for doing price action trading in Nifty.

`20 EMA:` It is the fastest way to know whether the price action is still trending or has stopped trending.
- Price action is trending when price touches 20 EMA and bounces off it everytime.
- Price action has stopped trending when price successively breaches 20 EMA everytime and 20 EMA is becoming flat.
- In consolidation phase, price breaches every time it touches it. You can use 20 EMA to understand if the price has started trending.

`50 EMA:` this works beautifully on Nifty charts. It respects that line as a strong S/R line.

`100 EMA:`
- If the price bounces off 100 EMA then it acts as a good `Entry trigger` "in the direction of 200 EMA".
- If the price breaks 100 EMA then you take the view that the trend is changing in the near term and you stop buying the 100 EMA dip.

`200 EMA:` It indicates an established trend.
- If price is below 200 EMA its a bear trend and 200 EMA acts as a strong resistance. You only take Short trades in bear trend.
- If price is above 200 EMA its a bull trend and 200 EMA acts as a strong support. You only take Long trades in bull trend.
- When the price breaches 200 EMA the current ongoing trend has been invalidated.
- Once the trend reverses, you start taking trades the other way.
- But instead of reversing if the price sideways and chopes 200 EMA for a certain length of time then market is in accumulation phase and is conslidating.

`20 EMA, 100 EMA and 200 EMA:` It indicates a strong accumulation phase When all 3 EMAs run flat in an interleaved/parallel fashion.
