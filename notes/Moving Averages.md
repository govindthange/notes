# Buying Dips using MA

On your way up you buy the dip at 10 Daily SMA/EMA.
If the price breaks below 20 SMA/EMA then you take the view that the trend is changing in the near term and you stop buying the dip.
Once the trend reverses, you totally stop going long and trade the other way.
