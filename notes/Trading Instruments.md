### Trading Plan

- Keep `Stop Loss:` at the risk of 0.25% or lower.
- Take `25% Profit` at `1 X of Stop Loss` (call it A). 
- After Take Profit #1 move the `Stop Loss A` to `Break Even` (call it B).
- Take `50% Profit` at `1.5 X of Stop Loss A`
- Trail your S.L. at newer S/R levels, Pivot levels or Trendlines (Refer it as C, D...).
- Wait for the market to stop you through Stop Loss.
