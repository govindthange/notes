## Swing High

It is identified as the highest point displayed in a given time period.

## Swing Low

A swing low is created when the low is lower than any other point over a given time period.

# Mark swings
[...](https://youtu.be/2h5ryPi6ZYo?t=209)

On one level higher timeframe of your trading timeframe, mark trend like so:

## Step 1. Spot an impulsive move

Start from an initial point and wait for the price to cross a previous structural high/low (if any).

## Step 2. Mark the new major swing level.

Spot the new structural high as a major swing level like so:
1. First, wait for the candles to `cross the previous strucutural high/low` (if any).
2. Next, wait for at least `2 retracement candles` following the highest-high or lowest-low candle as identified in step #1 above.
3. Designate the highest-high or lowest-low candle, from the price move in step 1 above, as a major swing high/low.
	- Mark it with a horizontal-ray tool in tradingview.

## Step 3. Wait for a throwback/pullback

Wait for a proper throwback/pullback to occur.

1. Just `observe` the price action.
	- The price may just continue going down/up/sideways before you declare it as a valid swing.
	- At this stage you can't identify whether the price action is a throwback/pullback as it may just continue in one direction turning into a reversal.
- Wait until the price shows a relatively `deep throwback/pullback` (not a shallow one).

## Step 4. Await recovery from the throwback/pullback

Wait for the price to `recover from the throwback/pullback` as the price might just continue in the direction of the throwback/pullback and invalidate the swing setup.

## Step 5. Mark the next major swing level

1. Next, after recovering from the previous swing's throwback/pullback, wait for the price to break the previous structural high/low.
	- Its only after the breakout you can mark the bottom/top of the throwback/pullback as a major swing level.
2. The moment previous structural high (or low) is broken, you can mark the recent low (or high) caused by throwback (or pullack) as the `Major Swing Level` ^d0b31a
	- Anything between the major swing levels are `Minor Swing Levels`
	- The throwback/pullback level is important only to know when the trend reverses in future.
3. Wait for the price to make a `new structural high/low`.
4. Repeat [[#Step 2 Mark the new major swing level]].

# Trading Swings

- Enter the trade in Step 5.2 i.e. soon after the structural high is broken. Refer [[#^d0b31a]]
- `Stop Loss` can be a few points below the prevoius swing high/low or a few points below the throwback/pullback level. ==<-- WIP==
