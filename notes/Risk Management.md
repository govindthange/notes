> Always trade from the position of strength. Do not get wrecked!

Before entering a trade most of the retail traders wait for a confirmation signal and then enter trade with a tight S.L. set too close to their entry price. They get immediately taken out in the noise.

Institutional Traders use Fibonacci Levels, Trend Lines, Simple S&R Levels to actually look for confluences to try and place their orders. They use [[Average True Range]] to determine S.L. Put your stop loss 2.5 times of ATR.

# Being Stop Hunted
How to avoid being stop hunted?

## Putting tight stops

The tighter the stop loss, the more likely it will get triggerred!

> The Stop Loss should be placed at a point where the premise of the trade is negated if you get stopped.

Stop loss should only be set a point where your strategy is completely invalidated. Say you have spotted a Double Top and you have put a S.L. above the Double Top level then if the Stop Loss does get hit it should only mean that the Double Top pattern was invalid. Stop Loss should not be at a point at which once hit, the price continues downward thereby confirming your Double Top was indeed valid.

## Putting stops at the same place as everybody else.

Market hunts for liquidity. They are not the cartel as such pinging your stops.

### Put your stop far enough

The trouble with putting S.L. too far out of the way is the risk-reward getting skewed.

### Let the stops get stopped out and then get in.

Let the other retailers get stopped out first and then enter when price comes under your strategy, where it still is valid.

### Enter where people put their stops.

Put your Limit order for entering the trade at the very point where other retailers put stop loss.

Essentially you are taking advantage of other's S.L by entering with the S.L. while putting your own stop far away from others.

# Quit moving stops too soon

Stop moving your S.L.  to breakeven / takeprofit too soon! 

The beginning stages of a trade is important so don't move S.L. in the initial stages of the trade. Take off is the most crucial part so you must leave enough room for the price to breath.

Give price enough rooom to oscillate and absorb noise before it makes further gains. Let the time pass by and after that gradually moveup, but not immeidately after entering the trade.

Follow these approaches after entering a trade:

- To move S.L. consider using [[Indicators#Volatility Indicators]] like [[Indicators#Average True Range]] to determine next S.L. so that you leave enough room for your trade to breath before it makes new highs/lows.
- Wait for price to form new S&L level and then `move your S.L. to the new S&R level`. Think as if it was your new entry point.
- If you are really fearful about being stopped out, scale out of your posistion. `Exit some part of your trade` instead of moving S.L.
- You may choose to `move some part of your S.L.` instead of moving all of it.

## S.L. Moving Strategy

- As soon your position reaches 1 X of the S.L do as follows:
- Take out 25% of the profit
- Move S.L. to the `Break Even` point
- Optionally advace the `Take Profit` level from 1.5 times to 2 times of the original Stop Loss level.
