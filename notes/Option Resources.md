[Tom Sosnoff's must know list](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nLG2IIRacl8)

1. Understanding how `derivatives strategies` work is the one place you can go to make tons of decisions to improve your decision-making skills, which ultimately, the number of decisions you make has a direct relationship with how successful you are.
2. Understanding `F&O market` is improtant because the futures business market drives the securities business. Most amount of leverage, most amount of liquidity.
3. Understanding [[Glossary#Pairs Trade | pairs trading]]
4. Understanding `yield curves` i.e. being able to articulate interest rates and the yield curve is important. When you get to a certain level everybody cares about interest rates and yet very few people can explain it.
5. Understanding how `volatility` works is crucial.  Understanding how volatility is a measure of fear, how it is the 1 SD expected move, and finally, how correlation plays a role w.r.t. premium and volatility is a very special skill to develop. Once you understand this you will be able to take advantage of it.
6. Understading `hedging` i.e. knowing that individual stocks/underlyings are very difficult to hedge and probably not worth it because the expected move is greater than the realized move, which means you have to overpay the price of the expected move as premium. Understand that volatilty is always overstated (since its a measure of fear) by the numbers and the actual volatility is not that much. Hedging is a portfolio thing and makes sense when done for the entire portfolio.
7. Understanding `DeFi` and all its aspects are important.
8. Understanding `premium selling` and all the mathematics is the key.
9. `Basis improvement` in the end is the key to long term scalable and repeatable success.
