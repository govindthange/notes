Period: 2 Hours

## Step 1. Scan for gappers

- Gappers are stocks that have gapped up or gapped down with a dramatic percentage overnight due to earnings, clinical trials, or other deals and agreements.
- There is likely to be an immediate reaction in the first two hours. Day traders look for momentum, volume, and valatility.

Tools:
- Benzinga Pro
- Trade Ideas Gap Scanner
- briefing.com: a web based service for live market commentary
- DAS Trader platform
- Thinkorswim pre market scanner

## Step 2. Analyze news for justifying gaps

Analyze the stock specific news in conjuction with its fundamentals to justify the gap-up/down and potential follow through. And if there is no news at all, thinking what may have caused the gap; is it regulatory related?

### Collect the news

For each gapper go through all the published news headlines from reliable resources.

### Categorize the news

Catregorize news as follows:
1. Positive Catalyst: Good earnings, deals & buyout rumors, mergers & agreements with potential transaction price.
2. Negative Catalyst: Fallout of a deal/partnership, global economic crisis, whether a stock is issuing offering or getting delisted.
3. Fluff News: News disguised as "positive news", Penny stock companies using positive wording in PR like "potential deal", "engagement", "optimistic outlook", "in the talk", "possible partnership". Anything in gray that something can happen but may not happen too. Technically it is not a lie but not a possibility too.
4. No News: if there is no news justifying the gaup-up/down. For example in US a gap can occur due to a T12 halt and not any news.

Remaining 3 stpes are about turning gappers into a trading plan.

## Step 4. Analyze key data points
[...](https://youtu.be/hH65uE4COec?t=301)

You can't buy every single stock gap-up that has news. Also having a ticker and a headline is not enough.

If you are trading small cap penny stocks then look for the following key data point on stocks:

- Daily charts
- Price levels
- Float
- Market cap
- Short interest
- Financials metrics
- SEC filings

You need not anlayze this if you are trading big companies.

## Step 5. Compiling all data into a short term trading plan

Use your past experience, your screen time, and current market sentiments to plan out the most likely scenario to happen short term i.e. look at the intraday timeframe and make educated guesses on what the stock could do to the upside/downside.

Answering questions like:
1. Was it a gapper?
2. Does it have valid news?
3. Did you draw key levels?
	- Draw the key resistance lines for knowing where the stock could potentially go to if there is enough momentum, volume and hype.
	- Draw the key support lines where the stock could potentially dip down to and consolidate in that area before moving the next leg up.
4. Did you ensure that the stock is not a low float? It may be small cap but not a sketch penny stock. It also has a bigger market cap so there could be funds and other institutions playing it.

Look at the past behavior of the selected gapper i.e. how the stock has traded intraday you can check whether it likes to push fast in the moring and quickly pullback? or it sells off and go read on the day first before consolidating and volume coming back in? [...](https://youtu.be/hH65uE4COec?t=535)

Finally you can make a Long/Short trading plan even before the market opens up.

Each stock should take 5-10 min. If you look at SEC filing it may take even longer.
