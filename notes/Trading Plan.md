# Growing Small Trading Account
[...](https://youtu.be/WdcqKUUJIA4?t=82)

> Trading is about putting on positions and manage those positions until they are winners.

## Rules

- `Trading Account Capital` = $2000 - $5000
- `Trade Size` < 3% to 5% of your `Trading Account Capital` (below $500) <-- [[Money Management#Calculations]]
- Choose from the list of most liquid stocks. [[Stock Selection]]
- Define the risk.
- Create positive decay.
- `Probability of Success` > 50%

### Recommended Strategies

#### Sell a `Put Spread`

- `Sell a put` to `collect premium`.
- `Buy a put` at lower strike to `define risk` while `lowering margin` or buying power reduction (`BPR`)

> By reducing margin we do give up some potential income, but we also reduce risk, allowing us to remain consistent with our overall trade plan.

##### Example
[...](https://youtu.be/WdcqKUUJIA4?t=637)

Experts prefer selling a Naked Put when volatility is cheap as you get paid more for high volatility.

## Hurdles

- Staying active and sticking to the plan of maintaining and monitoring 5-10 small positions in a small trading account can prove to be a challenge in absence of a solid foundation deeply rooted in the rules & principals.
	- Do the oppsite of "Trade smart not often" i.e. "Just do stuff for the sake of doing stuff".
	- Put on as many small positions as you can because...
		- Its the only way to get better.
		- Its the only way to maintain activity.
		- Its the only way to stick to the plan.
- Keeping expectations down especially during the period when the market has low volatility.
- Keeping positions small and no more than 4% of `Trading Account Capital` per trade.

## Collateral

- Grows MF and securities capital

## Trading Portfolio

### Monthly Trade

- Grows collateral
- Covers weekly trade loss
- Big trading capital
- Very high win rate
- Low R/R

### Weekly Trade

- Grows monthly trade capital
- Covers intraday loss
- Medium sized trading capital
- High win rate
- Decent R/R

### Intraday Trade

- Grows weekly trading capital
- Smallest trading capital
- Good win rate
- Good R/R
