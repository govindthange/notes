# Strategy

> The more you sweat in times of peace, the less you bleed in times of war.

If I was hard on myself during training then the tournament will become easy on me. If I was easy on myself during training then the tournament would be hard on me.

# Mindset

## History of Winning

A mindset of confidence.

See yourself as a winner who had a history of winning.

1. Go back to your junior school, high school and college days and recall all the successes, however small they may be, you have had.
2. ist them down on a paper.
3. Read them over and over again.
	- Do it till it ingrains in you.
	- Do it till you believe that you have had a history of winning
	- Do it till you believe you that winning is what you do. It is who you are. And this tournament is just another example of how you win.


# Small Goals

Set small achievable goals throughout the day.

Say you set 10 goals.
Now do everything to achieve all the goals for the day.
Say you achieve 10 goals for the day and you do that throughout the week. By the end of the week you would have achieved 70 goals.
By the end of month you would have achieved 300 goals.
By the end of year it will be 3600 goals.

## Consequence

A navy seal will always face a consequence for not hitting a goal.

Do what you hate the most when you fail to hit a goal.
