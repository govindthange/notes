# Quarterly Cycles

## Q1 (Jan - Mar)

- Its the ethereum season.
	- In the the 4th week of December or 1st week of January move your bitcoin capital to ethereum.
- Ethereum outperforms Bitcoin [...](https://youtu.be/mI0TpziASh4?t=1148)
	- Note that if ETH does not bleed against BTC in december then this thesis becomes invalid. ETH may or may not rally in Q1.

## Q2 (Apr - Jun)

- Its generally an altcoin season once bitcoin and ethereum finishes their rally.
- Ethereum loses all its momentum by April.
- If bitcoin is above bull market support band and not rallying then move your bitcoin and ethereum position to alt coins.
- Use the bollinger bands to see whether altcoin is overvalued against bitcoin. The moment it starts touching the 2 deviation band then plan to exit the position. The 2 deviation is the "run for the hills level", get out of the position.

## Q3 (Jul - Sep)

- Bitcoin dumps in september.
- Accumulate bitcoin in this month.

## Q4 (Oct - Dec)

- Its the bitcoin season.
	- Bitcoin is the king. When the bitcoin runs market has to bow down to it and stay out of its way.
	- Exit your altcoin positions and put it into bitcoin.
	- In the 4th week of December or 1st week of January move money to Ethereum.
- Accumulation phase for ethereum. Dollar cost average ethereum in these 3 months.
- Bitcoin outperforms Ethereum [...](https://youtu.be/mI0TpziASh4?t=1148)
	- ETH bleeds against BTC in Q4 and then starts to rallying in January.
	- If ETH does not bleed against BTC in december then this thesis becomes invalid. ETH may or may not rally in Q1.
