## Analyzing Dominance (BTC.D)
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EADLE7kO4tA)

How much of the `Crypto Total Market Cap, $(CALCULATED BY TRADINGVIEW)` (CTMC) is just `BTC`?

If BTC.D is going down and CTMC is also going down then stay away from trading altogether because it suggests that we are on the brink of a dip.

If BTC.D is going down but CTMC is going up then it suggests two things:
- alt-coin season has begun.
- We are at the peak of a bull market cycle.

Once we establish where we are in terms of altcoin-bitcoin cycle the next step is to use technical analysis (weekly/monthly timeframe) to estimate how long this stage of the cycle could last.

## Analyzing behavior

### Weekly MA
[...](https://youtu.be/QcJOnlnbGeY?t=152)

#### 8 Week SMA

#### 20 Week MA

For bitcoin to trend up you need 3 things [...](https://youtu.be/QcJOnlnbGeY?t=210)
1. BTC stays above 20 Week MA.
2. [[Moving Averages#Golden Cross]] [...](https://youtu.be/zNfY8rGezwo?t=62)
3. Bitcoin holds the 20 Week MA as support.
	- Wicks on the daily timeframe don't matter.
		- On the daily frame its fine if you go below it.
	- Care about the weekly closes above the 20 Week MA.
	- We say bitcoin DID NOT HOLD 200 week when bitcoin opens and closes below the 20 Week MA.


Bitcoin must test 20 Week MA every 2 to 3 months because it rejuvenates the market.
If we don't test it for 4-5 months in a row, then when we do test it, it will be highly likely that we will not hold it as support and crash down.
