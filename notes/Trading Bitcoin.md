## Analyzing dominance (BTC.D)
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EADLE7kO4tA)

How much of the `Crypto Total Market Cap, $(CALCULATED BY TRADINGVIEW)` (CTMC) is just `BTC`?

If BTC.D is going down and CTMC is also going down then stay away from trading altogether because it suggests that we are on the brink of a dip.

If BTC.D is going down but CTMC is going up then it suggests two things:
- alt-coin season has begun.
- We are at the peak of a bull market cycle.

## Analyzing moving averages

### Pi Cycle Top Indicator (111DMA, 350DMA)
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2gNah0LEo4k) | [...](https://www.lookintobitcoin.com/charts/pi-cycle-top-indicator/)

- It is effective in picking out the timing of market cycle highs to within 3 days. It helps to time the market cycle top within 3 days before a significant correction.
- When the 111DMA moves up and crosses the 350DMA x 2 we see that it coincides with the price of Bitcoin peaking.
- It is useful to indicate when the market is very overheated.
	- So overheated that the shorter term moving average, which is the 111 day moving average, has reached a x2 multiple of the 350 day moving average.
	- Historically it has proved advantageous to sell Bitcoin at this time in Bitcoin's price cycles.

# DCA using risk metrics
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S7nB1z6fTBQ)

Just DCA in & out using the risk metrics created by Benjamin Cowen
- Do not just watch the market come and go.
- Do not try to time the top and bottom.
- If you keep waiting for the lower price then it may never come.
- Forget about the market volatility.
- People who play the long game and hodl must consider taking profits along the way.

Say you allocated x amount of capital then use risk metrics band like so:

## Below 0.3

- DCA in with $3x capital.
- The risk metrics has never gone below 0.12.
- If you only plan to buy at 0.2 then you will get a very few opportunities.

## 0.3 to 0.4

- DCA in with $2x capital.

## 0.4 to 0.5

- DCA in $x capital.
- As of 13 Oct 2021, the 56k price is at 0.5 risk metrics.

## Above 0.9

- DCA out by setting a trailing stop loss when risk metrics touches 0.9.
- The risk metrics has never gone beyond 0.94.
- Do not exit in Q4.
- When you do exit, move your capital to stable coins and altcoins.
