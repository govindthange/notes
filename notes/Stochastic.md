Stochastics displays the location of the closing price relative to the high and low range over a specific period of time.

Stochastic can be used when market shows `extreme momentum` in one direction and you are awaiting a throwback/pullback.

> Its best when you have missed the trend and want to avoid that risky chase but still get on board for that last squeeze of the momentum. So you set an alert on that first stochastic entry in OB/OS zone and look for entry after confluence from candlestics.

# Components

## Parameters
[...](https://tradingsim.com/blog/stochastic-rsi/)

The most common setting for the Stochastics oscillator is 14, 3, 3 or simply 14, 3. This simply indicates a 14-period look-back and a 3 period SMA for %K, which is %D.

## %K

- It represents current closing prices in relation to the defined high and low period.
- Its a fast moving blue line.

## %D

- Its a simple moving average of %K.
- Its a slow moving orange/red line.

## OB/OS Zones

- OB/OS Values:
	- Overbought Zone > 80
	- Oversold Zone < 20
- Overbought zone in a downtrend is potentially where the pullback could end.
- Oversold zone in an uptrend is potentially where a throwback could end.

## Momentum

- Extreme upward momentum is qualified by stochastic in overbought condition (>80).
- Extreme downward momentum is qualified by stochastic in oversold condition (<20).

## Crossovers

- When fast MA (blue) crosses above the slow MA (orange) then look for going long.
- When fast MA (blue) crosses below the slow MA (orange) then look for going short.

## Over/Under MA

- When fast MA (blue) is above the slow MA (orange) its bullish. Both lines are rising.
- When fast MA (blue) is below the slow MA (orange) its bearish. Both lines are declining.

# Using Stochastic
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=THKFlE3119E)

- Time when the pullback/throwback will end in an existing trend.
	- In a downtrend wait for the overbought level to time a short position at a pullback level.
	- In an uptrend wait for the oversold level to time a long position at a throwback level.
- Filter your trading setup through OB/OS levels in higher timeframes.
	- If higher timeframe (Daily Chart) shows oversold it hints to have a long bias os you should not go short in a shorter timeframe (1 Hour Chart).
- Serve it as an entry trigger i.e. as a confirmation for entering a trade.
	- Use this as a confluence factor to build up a long/short bias.
	- When stochastic rises above 20 level then go long
	- When stochastic falls below 80 level then go short

# Stochastic as a filter
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V6n2GzGlDZM)

1. [ ] Check the trend.
2. [ ] Check the momentum i.e. stochastic showing `continuous` overbought/oversold conditions.
	- Note that you want `a long continuous` prior OB/OS levels not a short burst stay in OB/OS zones. [...](https://youtu.be/i9QzG5PCXWA?t=411)
3. [ ] Check the strength i.e. price action shows large bodied `candles in one direction`.
4. [ ] Wait for `the first time stochastic enters` OB (or OS) condition against an initial moementum i.e. after a prior continuous OS (or OB) condition (or vice versa).
	- Note that it does not work well when the stochastic enters the zone the 2nd or 3rd time.
5. [ ] You may _optionally_ wait for the fast MA crossing over the slow MA
6. [ ] Finally you can use #4 and/or #5 as a `confluence` for going long
	- Go long upon receiving confirmation from a candlestick pattern
	- Go long upon a successful test at S/R or trendline i.e. support becoming resistance or vice versa.

#### Example

- Price shows a good upward momentum.
- Stochastic confirms extended momentum by showing a continuous overbought condition.
- Momentum begins to unwind i.e. price starts to rotate down slightly. This hints a possible change in momentum so you need to look for going long in that first oversold condition.
- Wait for `the first oversold condition` after you have seen a continuous overbought condition. [...](https://youtu.be/i9QzG5PCXWA?t=155)
- You may further want to wait for crossover where fast MA crosses above the slow MA and then go long.

# Guidelines
[...](https://youtu.be/i9QzG5PCXWA?t=411)

- Do not blindly trade OB/OS signals. Use it in conjuction with trend and other confluence factors.
	- In a downtrend wait for the overbought level to time a short position at the pullback level. Short only after receiving an entry trigger like bearish reversal candlestick pattern.
	- In an uptrend wait for the oversold level to time a long position at the throwback level. Long only after receiving an entry trigger like bullish reversal candlestick pattern.
- Do not trade against the trend. Use it to time the pullback.
- Wait for `a long continuous` prior OB/OS levels not a short burst stay in OB/OS zones. [...](https://youtu.be/i9QzG5PCXWA?t=411)
- It only works best with the first OB/OS condition against the initial momentum. It does not work well when the stochastic enters in the 2nd or 3rd time.
