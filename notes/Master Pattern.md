## Contraction (Value)

- AKA `Consolidation Phase` or `Choppy Sideways Movement`
- Caused by low institutional volume. The range has confirmed that it is getting smaller. (Mostly sideways movement)
- You do not want to trade in this phase.
- It is the most important phase because it defines value. There is an average price that comes out of a contract zone that draws forward on the chart  and defines the future value price (True Value) that both buyers and sellers have agreed upon in the past.

## Expansion (Price Exploration)

-  AKA `Wipsaw Breakout`
-  Price leaves the Contract phase and starts exploring higher and lower prices by oscillating back and forth.
-  You see increasing institutional volume. Range stars opening up, taking out highs and lows.
-  This phase offers very good entry points.
-  Smart money accumulates positions at discounted prices.
-  Dumb money (paper hands) gets beaten up. Everytime you jump you get crushed.

![[The3MarketCycle_ContractionAndExpansion.png]]

## Trend (Sustained Deviation from Value)

- AKA `Profit Taking` phase or `The Pay-out Cycle`
- Insitutional traders start taking profits.
- It has Retrace/Mini Cycles.
- The market makes its move to higher or lower prices.Once it gets there the market will establish a new contraction point which eventually leads to the 3 phases again.
- Institutional traders use FOMO inspired liquidity to exit their previously accumulated positions.
- This is where most retail traders make "temporary profits"

![[The3MarketCyclePhases_v2.png]]

1. These 3 phases complete 1 market cycle.
2. At any time current price can be classified as in one of these 3 phases.
3. They can be seen on every timeframe chart from the 1 minute to the monthly.
4. Your effectiveness as a trader depends on your ability to recognize which of the 3 phases you are in and then interact with it correctly. In the beginning stay out of the contraction phase and try to understand the expansion phase. In the expansion phase you can get either beaten up or when you get good at it you can use it as insitutions use it.

![[The3MarketCyclePhases_Example.png]]

By understanding what phase the market is in, you can better expect when MA lines will be more effective according to your specific time-frame.

# The Master Pattern
[...](https://youtu.be/E-HRjjWQk6M?t=1435)
[...](https://youtu.be/BACYi2sSJY4?t=428)

It is the smart money business model.

By understanding `The Master Pattern` you can understand what phase the market is in currently.

The Master Pattern is one single price pattern that embodies all market participants `trading activity and behavior`. It is the gold standard for tracking `price-action-defined market value`.

The 2 Chart Setup (either 4H:15M or 1D:4H)

## Higher Timeframe Chart (Directional Bias)

- Create a `directional bias` for buying/selling opportunity
- Spot `contraction` phase
- Draw the `average true price` like so:
	- First draw a box engulfing the price movement.
	- Project forward the price line cutting through the middle of the box.
- See where the `price is settling`. Whether it is settling short or long.
- Understand that 4H is the authority over the smaller timeframe.
	- If the 4H is entering a `trend phase` to the downside then every timeframe lower than the 4H chart will have all the big moves towards the downside.
	- Since we know the superior timeframe is trending down, which is confirmed by the price action being under the average price, we are going to short the expansion legs for the most optimal entries into the prevailing trend in lower timeframe chart.

## Lower Timeframe Chart (Entry Chart)

### Analyze

- Spot `contraction` phase.
- Draw a box around contraction phase by projecting forward the price line.

### Enter

- Everything above the contract phase is going to be shorting opportunity. So you will trade when price is on the counter trend side of your last average price on your smaller entry chart. Try to find a rotation point back towards average price and jump in.
- So while tracking price in lower timeframe, everytime you get above the contraction phase you will add to your short position.
- You will keep shorting till the fundamental direction on the higher timeframe reverses. Keep track of what the 4H chart is doing right now. Is it rotating its cycle?

### Exit when right

When your higher timeframe gets back to your average priceleve thats the time to exit.

- Once price has returned back through average price in trend with your higher timeframe.
- If price spikes and puts you into major profit, stay in until the market enters the contraction phase again.

### Exit when wrong

If your supporting higher timeframe rotates against you confirming that the actual trend has not started yet and you are still in the expansion phase get out and re-evalute.

# Tips

- The very first entry opportunity when the move starts in the whole market cycle is the best opportunity and have the highest probability of getting paid.
- If you only focus on identifying the very first opportunity in every major move and trade just that it will be very successful.
