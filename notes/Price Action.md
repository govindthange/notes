Price action is about trading in the momement real time i.e. how hard the stock rejects a resistance or how hard it bounces off a  support.

When you are staring at indicators all the time you are not observing the little nuances in the price action regarding who is trapped in pain and where the momentum be potentially be going. Price action is the only indicator that does not lag and gives the best anticipation and confirmation when picking entries/exits. ^4a4d7a

Learn to look at [[Candlestick Patterns]] by itself without relying on any [[Indicators]]. It will give you a better read of a stock and speed up learning.
