## Indicators 

Confirming the entry, tracking progress, and exiting the trade when wrong and riding the profit when right.

### Trend Indicators

Analyze the direction and strength of the trend based on the past pirice data. Since its based on the past data the singals are given after the trend ahas already been establish.

- Moving Averages
- MACD
- Parabolic SAR
- Average Directional Movement Index

### Momentum Indicators

Measure the relative strength of the recent price moves and plot a value between 0 and 100. If the price rises strongly then oscillator follows and reaches the overbought level giving a sell signal. Similarly if the price falls, the oscillator reaches an oversold level giving a buy signal.

- [[RSI | Relative Strength Indicator]]
- Stochastic RSI
- Momentum Indicator

### Volume Indicators

Measure the strength of the price move by measuring trading volume data. Following are popular amongs forex traders.

- OBV
- Chaikin Oscillator

### Volatility Indicators

Measure the rate at which the price is changing regardless of its direction. The indicator value rise when the price moves fast and falls when the price moves slowly.

- Bollinger Bands
- Average True Range (ATR)
