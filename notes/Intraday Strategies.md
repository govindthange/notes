# Booming Bulls
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8OurAiVyhE4)

## Setup

Timeframe
- 5M (Trading)

Indicators
- Day High/Low Lines
- Pivots (Fibonacci, Daily)
	- R2, R1, P, S1, S2
	- Gives entry upon price cross
	- Target
- VWAP (Defaults)
	- Filter trade
	- If price below VWAP then have a short bias
	- If price above VWAP then have a long bias
- Stochastic RSI (Default)
	- Only for extra confirmation
	- Buy when blue line crosses above the red line
	- Sell when blue line crosses below the red line

## Entry

Trading Time
- Start at 10:45 AM

Enter the trade
- with VWAP bias
- when price breaks
	- VWAP line or
	- Pivot level or
	- Day's High or
	- Day's Low or
	- [[Market Structure]] (i.e. 5M trendline or S/R) or
	- Major [[Swing]] High/Low
- upon receiving stochastic RSI confirmation

## Exit

Stop Loss
- A few pips away on the other side of the vwap.
- If vwap is too far then nearby S/R level.
- If VWAP and S/R are too far then avoid the trade.

Exit the trade
- when price reaches
	- Next pivot level
	- Or 1%
