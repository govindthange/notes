# Market Lingo/Jargons

#### Short
A short may mean "insufficient supply". Shorting or Short Selling may mean selling more of something than you have. In short selling you sell something that you don't have i.e. you borrow a stock before selling and becomes short of the same. You are obligated to buy back the stock later.

#### DCA
Dollar Cost Averaging. Buying instruments at regular intervals rather than timing the market.

#### DCA'ing Out
Booking profit at regular intervals rather than waiting for tops & bottoms to book profit.

#### Overshoot

#### Undershoot

#### Gaps
##### Breakaway gap
The gap at the time of breakout

##### Exhaustion gap
The gap that ends an uptrend/downtrend.

#### Knot
A place in a strong [[Trend]] where price moves sideways (horizontally) for at least 3 days.

#### Pairs Trade
Pairs trade is buying something and selling something else that is highly correlated.
