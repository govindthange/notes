# Pullback Test

Monitor how price breaks market structure.

Wait patiently for the market to create a clear structure.
- 1st wait for the market to break the structure.
- then wait for the market to give you a clear level.
- then wait for the market to confirm that the level is strong.

- wait for a strong bullish/bearish candles into the structure.
- Then wait for a retest.
	- Either wait for the candle to continue in the direction of the trend. 
	- OR wait for the multiple wick rejections.

### Uptrend Pullback Retest

#### Pattern 1
[...](https://youtu.be/RAMgdqP4gr4?t=450)

- Strong `bearish candles` showing signs of bearish pressure into the key level.
- `Depletion` candles (candles with small body and/or long wicks).
- `Indecision` candle (dojis).
- Bullish `continuation` (bullish engulfing).

#### Pattern 2
[...](https://youtu.be/RAMgdqP4gr4?t=541)

- `Bearish candles` indicating exhaustion.
- Candles going down to the key level.
- Multiple candles forming `clusters of wick rejections`.
- `Bullish evidence`.

### Downtrend Pullback Retest

#### Pattern 1
[...](https://youtu.be/RAMgdqP4gr4?t=570)

- Strong `bullish candles` showing signs of bullish pressure into the key level.
- `Depletion` candles (candles with small body and/or long wicks).
- `Indecision` candle (dojis).
- Bearish `continuation` (Bearish engulfing).

#### Pattern 2
[...](https://youtu.be/RAMgdqP4gr4?t=608)

- `Bullish candles` indicating exhaustion.
- Candles going up to the key level.
- Multiple candles forming `clusters of wick rejections`.
- `Bearish evidence`.

### [[Area of Value]]
[...](https://youtu.be/RAMgdqP4gr4?t=709)

- Wait for the Bullish/Bearish Evidence.
- Wait for the 2nd Bullish/Bearish confirmation of the clear new level formation.
- Enter the trend.

# Trading Pullback Levels

Just because price made a pullback towards a key level does not qualify for entering a trade. Price may still breakthrough a certain [[#Pullback Levels]] and continue further.

### [[RSI]]
[...](https://youtu.be/cKKLujAdvzk?t=482)

- First change `Upper Band` and `Lower Band` settings of RSI to 50.
- Price retraces back to a key [[#Pullback Levels]]
- To confirm whether the price will get rejected at the key level and reverses wait for the RSI to cross the middle line.

##### Examples

- Lets say the price breaks a key resistance, pulls back to retest it as a new support refer RSI. If RSI value crosses above the 50 line then its a good confirmation for a LONG position.

- Lets say in a downtrend the price pulls back and tests the trendline. There is also a confluence of an earlier support becoming a resistance. This is the `Area of Confluence`. Here you can wait for the RSI to cross the 50 level line down for a SHORT position.
