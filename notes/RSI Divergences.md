> RSI Divergence does NOT take precedence over the Market Structure (Trend, S&R Levels, Top-Down Multi-Timeframe Analysis). Its just to indicate you what to expect ahead of time. [...](https://youtu.be/KvZ7TH0iYz4?t=1844)

Usually you will start by marking tops and bottoms on the price swings. For all these tops and bottoms you would look for divergences associated with them.

Essentially a reversal is a break in the market structure. Example: A higher high is broken into higher lows. You must wait enough to see this being played out before making a trend.

Anticipate a divergence w/o referring RSI like so:
- Expect a bearish divergence when 2+ red candles begin interleaving after a set of consecutive green candles.
- Expect a bullish divergence when 2+ green candles begin interleaving after a set of consecutive red candles.

There are 3 types of divergences viz Regular, Hidden, and Exaggerated.
- `Regular Divergences (R)` predicts a potential reversal in a trend because the price action loses its steam.
- `Hidden Divergences (H)` predicts the beginning of a strong momentum in the direction of the existing trend.
- `Exaggerated Divergences (E)`

Once you approach an Area of Value while in a trend then use below table to predict continuation or reversal in price trend.

| Divergence    | Uptrend     | Downtrend   |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| Bullish (H/R) | HL X LL (H) | LL X HL (R) |
| Bearish (H/R) | HH X LH (R) | LH X HH (H) |

- To spot a bearish divergence (R/H) focus on the `tops`; the `swing highs` on the price chart and RSI.
- To spot a bullish divergence (R/H) focus on the `botttoms`; the `swing lows` on the price chart and RSI.

- Use divergences to reliably predict directional move in the direction of the trend.

# Predicting trend reversals w/ Regular Divergences

## Regular Bearish Divergence
[...](https://youtu.be/VwVEVu0-JWQ?t=272)

Predict the `uptrend reversal by comparing swing highs` in the price chart with swing highs in the RSI.

`Price Chart:` Higher Highs

`RSI:` Lower Highs or Flat <-- Loss of upward momentum

`Analysis:` A possible reversal to the downside.

`Strategy:` Enter a short posisition upon confirmation.

`Confirmation:`
- Wait for a bearish reversal candlestick pattern like a shooting star.
- Wait for the RSI to go below the 50% to confirm the trend has actually reversed.
- You may also wait for a retest on a moving average to gain more confidence.


`Entry Trigger:` Draw a trendline touching all the higher lows. As the **price breaks down the trendline** while showing a **bearish divergence** on the RSI, wait for the **candle to close below the trendline** followed by a **retest move** and only then enter a short position.

`Stop Loss:` The high point of the wick of the previous or current candle.

`Target:` 2x of the S.L.

## Regular Bullish Divergence

Predict the `downtrend reversal by comapring swing lows` in the price chart with swing lows in the RSI.

`Price Chart:` Lower Lows

`RSI:` Higher Lows or Flat <-- Loss of downward momentum

`Analysis:` A possible reversal to the upside.

`Strategy:` Enter a long posisition upon confirmation.

`Confirmation:`
- Wait for a bullish reversal candlestick pattern like a hammer etc.
- Wait for the RSI to go above the 50% to confirm the trend has actually reversed.
- You may also wait for a retest on a moving average to gain more confidence.

`Entry Trigger:` Draw a trendline touching all the lower highs. As the price **breaks above the trendline** while showing a **bullish divergence** on the RSI, wait for the **candle to close above the trendline** followed by a **retest move** and only then enter a long position.

`Stop Loss:` The lowest point of the wick of the previous or current candle.

`Target:` 2x of the S.L.

Trend is your friend so if you are new prefer trading hidden divergences over regular divergences.

## Hidden Bullish Divergence
[...](https://youtu.be/VwVEVu0-JWQ?t=375)

Predict the `uptrend continuation by comparing swing lows` in the price to swing lows in the RSI.

`Market Structure:` Uptrend

`Price Chart:` Higher Lows

`RSI:` Lower Lows <-- become increasingly oversold (below 50% RSI line)

`Analysis:` A possible continuation of uptrend.

`Strategy:` Enter a long posisition upon confirmation.

`Confirmation:` Await a bullish reversal candlestick pattern to show up.

`Entry Trigger:`

`Stop Loss:` The lowest point of the wick of the previous or current candle.

`Target:` 2x of the S.L.


As the upward momentum increases price moves up. Subsequently as the price relaxes and comes down it may actually turn more oversold on the RSI as the RSI makes lower lows below the 50% mark.

## Hidden Bearish Divergence

Predict the `downtrend continuation by comparing swing highs` in the price to swing lows in the RSI.

`Market Structure:` Downtrend

`Price Chart:` Lower Highs

`RSI:` Higher Highs <-- become increasingly overbought (above 50% RSI line)

`Analysis:` A possible continuation of downtrend.

`Strategy:` Enter a short posisition upon confirmation.

`Confirmation:` Await a bearish reversal candlestick pattern to show up.

`Entry Trigger:`

`Stop Loss:` The highest point of the wick of the previous or current candle.

`Target:` 2x of the S.L.

As the downward momentum increases price moves down. Subsequently as the price relaxes and pulls back to the upside it may actually turn more overbough on the RSI as the RSI makes higher highs above the 50% mark.

# Trading Divergences in discretionary style

1. A defined [[Market Structure]]
2. RSI signals OB/OS.
3. RSI Divergence
4. A big price move confirming playing out of RSI divergence.
5. A consolidation period after the big move.
6. A breakout trade.

## A sample trade
[...](https://youtu.be/Kzfvhdw6bJ8?t=586)

1. You mark the market structure.
	- You identified that the market has just bottomed out.
2. Wait for the RSI to enter OB/OS level.
3. RSI signals overbought.
4. Now you wait for some divergences to occur.
5. You found the `HH X LH (R)` setup.
6. Your entry criteria is not yet met. So you still wait.
7. RSI goes below 70 and no more overbought. You keep obverving.
8. You get yet another higher-high on price chart.
9. You wait for the 2 candle retracement (a pullback).
10. You spotted 2 candle pullback and have a new swing high. RSI is overbought too.
11. You referred RSI and spotted a divergence.
12. Now you are primed for a reversal trade.
13. Market shows a big red candle down.
14. After a big move down, you wait for a little period of consolidation.
15. Once you have a consolidation, you wait for the breakout.
