# Identify Trend
[...](https://youtu.be/2h5ryPi6ZYo?t=209)

On one level higher timeframe of your trading timeframe, mark trend like so:

## Uptrend

### Spot Impulsive Move

- Inintial Point
- Spot the new structural high
- Wait for the pullback. You won't be able to spot it until you make into a new structural high. Before this happens, you may just continue going down or sideways.
- Wait for the price to break the previous structural high only then you can mark the bottom of the pullback as a major swing level.
- The moment previous structural high is broken, you can mark the recent low as the `Major Swing Level`
- Anything between the major swing levels are `Minor Swing Level`
- The pullback level is important only to know when the trend reverses.

Major Swing Levels are Higher Highs or Higher Lows
