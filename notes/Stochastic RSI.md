# Components
## Crossovers

- When fast MA (blue) crosses above the slow MA (orange) then look for going long.
- When fast MA (blue) crosses below the slow MA (orange) then look for going short.

### Crossover Confirmation
[...](https://youtu.be/iirCKT5RY5M?t=287)

20 and 80 are the important levels which confirms the crossover signal in `Stochastic RSI`.

- Cross with confirmation above 20
	- When fast MA (blue) crossses above the slow MA (orange) and stochastic RSI goes above 20 then go long.
	- Do take confluence from the [[Price Action]] before going long.
- Cross with confirmation below 80
	- When fast MA (blue) crosses beneath the slow MA (orange) and stochastic RSI goes below 80 then go short.
	- Do take confluence from the [[Price Action]] before going short.

#### Warning

It may not always work.
