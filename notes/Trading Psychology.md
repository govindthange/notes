
# Why people suck at trading?
[Ross Williams](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sSj57fpHlb4)

Avoiding behavioral traits of people who consistently lose in the market should be everyone's first goal (i.e. survival)

- Losing money can become habitual. Do not become lossaholic. `Know when to stop and reassess`.
- People who don't believe that `market can and will change`, are destined to fail.
- An unwillingness to `manage risks` always end badly.
- Willful ignorance is a cancer.
- The conscientious trader will out earn a lazy genius time and again.
	- If you have a work ethic, if you want to apply what you know, you will outperform an ideal genius.
- Education is just opening the door, work ethic and application is the rest. If you think there is a hack, go do something else.
- Skeptical people stay alive, cynical people suffer paralysis.
	- Skepticism is important. Being skeptical is what keeps people out of the pump & dump schemes and bubbles like altcoins and other stock market frauds.
	- Cynical people make your day longer and drain your energies from negativity. They are like "oh, its too hard...", "You were born 15 years before me..", "Oh, it was easy in 2000...", "I never had the information the way you do...".
	- There are no execuses. The job is actually simple. You get a piece of paper everyday with a number on the bottom and your job is to make that number bigger. Thats how simple it is. It does not mean its easy to get there but the idea is to cut all the crap.
	- Its about how to make more money than you started with.
- Worthless opinions lead to poor decisions which give worthless outcomes.
	- Opinoins on shares/alt-coins based on hunches are useless. You got to do the work.

Observe and emulate the habits of successful traders and inverstors.


Losing at trading and losing at life overall have something in common.

- Education & Understanding (Intelligence)
	- Research shows that one can always improve IQ by 15 points with adequate education.
- Idea Generation (Creativity)
- Application "of ideas" (Conscientiousnes & Common Sense)
	- Most people are better at generating ideas than they think. Its the applicatoin of those ideas (i.e. turning that thought bubble into something profitable) is where they really struggle.

Successful traders follow these principles. There is no hack! Can you run your life this way?

Education + Work Ethic + Application is the receipie for success.
