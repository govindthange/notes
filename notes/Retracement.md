Retracement is a short-term price correction during an overall lerger trend.
- It is a temporary price reversal, in an existing trend.
- It does not indicate change in direction of a long-term trend.
- It offers opportunity to profit by entering an existing trend at a better price.

# Trading retracements

## Step 1. Indentify the trend

Find a strong upward/downward trend.

## Step 2. Indentify the swings

Identify a swing-high and a swing-low. Refer [[Swing]].

## Step 3. Plot the fibonacci retracement levels

- In an uptrend plot fibonacci retracement levels from the previous swing-low to the prevoius swing-high.
- In a downtrend plot fibonacci retracement levels from the previous swing-high to the previous swing-low.
- 0.382, 0.5, and 0.618 are the key fibonacci retracement levels used by traders as an effecient entry point.

## Step 4. Wait for the retracement

- Look for 0.382, 0.5, and 0.618 fibonacci levels.
- Wait for price to approach these key levels.
- The popular levels work due to its self fulfilling prophecy.
- In an uptrend wait for the price to find its support at these levels.
- In a downtrend wait for the price to find its resistance at these levels.

## Step 5. Wait for the confirmation

- In an established trend price will retrace back to the popular fibonacci levels before continuing its movement in the direction of the trend.
- Wait for the confirmation that the price will move back in the direction of the trend.
	- Wait for the candle to close in the direction of the trend.
	- Use [[Candlestick Patterns]].
	- Use [[RSI Divergence Playbook]] to predict whether the price is losing steam to bounce off of the fibonacci level.
- If price moves beyond 0.5 then it might be signal that a trend reversal is about to happen.

## Step 7. Exit the trade

### Step 7.1 Place your stop-loss

- In an uptrend place your stop-loss just below the retracement level.
- In a downtrend place your stop-loss just above the retracement level.

### Step 7.2 Place your target-profit

#### Plot the fibonacci extension levels

- 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 1, 1.382, and 1.618 are the key fibonacci extension levels used by traders as an effecient entry point.
- Use extension level corresponding to the retracement level as a target profit.
	- If you enter at 0.382 retracement level then the take profit should be at the corresponding extension level, which is 1.382.
	- If you enter at 0.682 then take profit at 1.682 extension level.
