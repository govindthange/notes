# Entry Location

## Put entries where the masses put their Stop Loss
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c4GaJKprGEs)

- Institutional traders are ordered to **buy at good levels only or don't buy at all**.
- Retail traders, unfortunately, wait for a confirmation from their techincal chart patterns before entering a trade and then upon confirmation they enter a trade with a **tight** stop loss to protect their posistion.
- Note that orders from retailers are typically very small and therefore get immediately filled but insitutional trades are of high volume and its not easy for them to get filled. They need high liquidity which they get at Stop Loss levels.

![[Trade# Stop Loss]]

> The next time you trade, plan your normal "retail trader strategy" based on chart patterns and then **do exactly the opposite**. Buy/Sell at the point where you earlier planned to put a stop loss and put a stop loss where you planned to enter the trade and see the result.

# Entry Methods

`Trade Size` is 1% of the `Trading Account Capital`

![[Money Management#Position Size and Stop Loss]]

## Normal Entry

Enter with 100% `Trade Size`

## Step Entry
[...](https://youtu.be/lmuTmzFA9q0?t=1120)

`Step 1 Entry.`
- Enter a trade with 50% of the designated `Trade Size` when you are trading with 1% of your `Trading Account Capital`.
- Enter with 40% if you are trading with 2% of your `Trading Account Capital`.

`Step 2 Entry.` Upon a successful `Pullback Test` add position as follows:
- Add remaining 50% of the designated `Trade Size` if you trading with 1% of your `Trading Account Capital`.
- Add remaining 60% if you are trading with 2% of your `Trading Account Capital`.

Generally Trade Size should not be more than 1% of your entire `Trading Account Capital`. But if you are an advanced trader and feel confident then you can stretch your trade size to 2%.

Since `Breakout Trades` are riskier you should stick to 1% of your `Trading Account Capital`.

`Reversal Trades` are relatively less resikier and with little experience you may trade with upto 2% of your `Trading Account Capital` by taking `Stepped Entries`.
