# Bid-Ask Spread

A de-factor measure of liquidity in the market.

A Bid-Ask spread is the amount by which the [[Trade# Ask Price | Ask Price]] exceeds the [[Trade# Bid Price | Bid Price]] for an asset.

It is the difference between the highest price that a buyer is willing to pay and the lowest price that a seller is willing to accept.

[[Area of Value#Institutional Trading Strategies]]

## Bid Price
Represends the demand for an asset.

## Ask Price
Represents the supply for an asset.
