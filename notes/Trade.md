# Stop Loss

Retail Traders look for confirmation and their S.L. will be too close & tight and then get taken out in the noise.

Institutional Traders use Fibonacci Levels, Trend Lines, Simple S&R Levels to actually look for confluences to try and place their orders. They use [[Average True Range]] to determine S.L. Put your stop loss 2.5 times of ATR.

# Bid-Ask Spread

A de-factor measure of liquidity in the market.

A Bid-Ask spread is the amount by which the [[Trade# Ask Price | Ask Price]] exceeds the [[Trade# Bid Price | Bid Price]] for an asset.

It is the difference between the highest price that a buyer is willing to pay and the lowest price that a seller is willing to accept.

[[Trading Framework# Institutional Trading Strategy]]

## Bid Price
Represends the demand for an asset.

## Ask Price
Represents the supply for an asset.

# Market Makers & Market Takers
