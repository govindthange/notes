

# Rules
[...](https://youtu.be/PEHmf1a3rgQ?t=821)

1. Trade with a small quantity.
	1. The base has to be big (as like in a pyramid).
	2. So try to add more in the intial legs instead of adding more after a significant move.
	3. If the trade moves in your favor, initial leg gives the maximum profit.
2. Sit still when you are in a loss.
	1. Do not Dollar Cost Average the position.
	2. Be ready to take the loss on the first leg of pyramid trading.
3. Add in more quantity as the trade goes in your favor.
4. Don't mess up the average entry price.
	1. If you keep your base wide i.e. if you add bigger position in the initial leg of the trade then it will keep your average in control.
5. Trail your stop loss logically.
	1. Keep a wider stop loss when intiating the trade.
	2. As you enter subsequent legs, trail the stop loss.
