A common street axiom: "Rising prices need high volume, but falling prices can decline on their own weight." ^8ed42c

A low volume on the way up indicates little buying pressure to push price higher there by resulting in fading momentum.

# Volume Indicator

> It shows the total assets traded on every single candle.

It measures how much of a given financial asset has traded in a specific period of time (i.e. on a single candle session).
- For stocks, volume measures the total number of shares traded in a single candle session.
- For futures, volume measures the total number of contracts traded in a single candle session.

## Study price moves

- Study the `strengths of advances and declines` in the asset.
- Understand the general `market strength or weakness`.

## Predict price moves

### Predict exhaustion moves

short period (price spike up/down + volume spike => volume drop)

A `sharp price move` combined with a simultaneous `volume rise`.
- Subsequently `volume decrease` after a spike.
- Volume may shift in the upcoming days, weeks, months or even years depending on the period analyzed.

They signal the potential `end of a current trend`.
- Traders who waited too long to participate in the move’s beginning are then afraid of missing more of the move and therefore move to pile in at market tops.
- This action exhausts the number of buyers, hence the name.
- Furthermore, volatility and increased volume can be a result of falling prices attempting to force out multiple traders when the amount of participants has become too much.
- This happens at market bottom.

### Predict reversals

long period (little price move + heavy volume) => trend reversal

A `long price move` either higher or lower, when there is `little price movement`, but `heavy volume`.

### Predict breakouts vs fakeouts

short period (price breakout + high volume) => strong move

- A `rise in volume` in coalition with an initial `breakout from a range`, or any other form of `chart pattern`, indicates a `strength in the move`.

short period (price breakout + low volume) => a weak fakeout move

- A `little change` in the volume or even `decreasing volume` on a `breakout`, it indicates a `lack of interest` and more potential for a `false breakout` to occur.
