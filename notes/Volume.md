A common street axiom: "Rising prices need high volume, but falling prices can decline on their own weight." ^8ed42c

A low volume on the way up indicates little buying pressure to push price higher there by resulting in fading momentum.

- Volume is the number of shares exchanged in a single transaction.
- Each histogram bar in the indicator shows the total volume traded for a given time period or for a certain price level.

# Volume Indicator

> It is volume by time showing total assets traded on every single candle.

It measures how much of a given financial asset was traded in a specific period of time (i.e. on a single candle session).
- For stocks, volume measures the total number of shares traded in a single candle session.
- For futures, volume measures the total number of contracts traded in a single candle session.

> Volume in options are typically not used to confirm trends in an underlying security.

## Study price moves

- Study the `strengths of advances and declines` in the asset.
- Understand the general `market strength or weakness`.

## Predict price moves

### Validating moves and trends

- Rising volume is a confirmation of an uptrend/downtrend.
- Higher-than-average volume lends credibility, and can help confirm price moves.
	- Moves in price on higher-than-average volume are more valid than moves on lower-than-average volume.
- Conversely, price moves on lower-than-average volume are thought to be less credible as they lack the participation from traders/investors.
	- Traders might view a break above resistance on lower-than-average volume with sskepticism due to lack of participation.

### Predict exhaustion moves

short period (price spike up/down + volume spike => volume drop)

A `sharp price move` combined with a simultaneous `volume rise`.
- Subsequently `volume decrease` after a spike. That is all of the buying/selling has exhausted.
- Volume may shift in the upcoming days, weeks, months or even years depending on the period analyzed.

They signal the potential `end of a current trend`.
- Traders who waited too long to participate in the move’s beginning are then afraid of missing more of the move and therefore move to pile in at market tops.
- This action exhausts the number of buyers, hence the name.
- Furthermore, volatility and increased volume can be a result of falling prices attempting to force out multiple traders when the amount of participants has become too much.
- This happens at market bottom.

### Predict reversals

long period (little price move + heavy volume) => trend reversal

A `long price move` either higher or lower, when there is `little price movement`, but `heavy volume`.

### Predict breakouts vs fakeouts

short period (price breakout + high volume) => strong move

- A `rise in volume` in coalition with an initial `breakout from a range`, or any other form of `chart pattern`, indicates a `strength in the move`.

short period (price breakout + low volume) => a weak fakeout move

- A `little change` in the volume or even `decreasing volume` on a `breakout`, it indicates a `lack of interest` and more potential for a `false breakout` to occur.

# Fixed Volume Profile (FVP)
[...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hImc9rBSSs)

> It is volume by price showing total assets traded at specific price levels for the specified duration.

- It shows `the most traded price` over a selected timeframe.
- It is volume by price level.
- Volume profile shows [[Area of Value]] for planning trades.

Point of Control (PoC)

- It is the line indicating the highest historic volume shown by the histogram.
- The larger the historic volume the thicker the trading zone will be. It is not just that thin red line (PoC).
- The buyers and sellers are going to put their orders around (not "at") the price zones corresponding to the high historic volume.
- This zone acts as S/R in future.

## Trading FVP

### Entering

Plot it across the swing move including the subsequent retracement.
- From swing low to swing high and including the subsequent throwback.
- From swing high to swing low and including the subsequent pullback.

### Exiting
[...](https://youtu.be/8hImc9rBSSs?t=740)

Plot it across the last retracement movement.
- In an up swing with a throwback, plot it from the last leg down (the throwback part).
- In a down swing with a pullback plot it from the last leg up (the pullback part).
