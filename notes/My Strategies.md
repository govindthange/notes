# Short Trades

## EMA + RSI Divergences + RSI OB/OS Cross

### Step 1. Highlight the [[Market Structure]]

Mark trends and nearby S/R levels that can potentially obstruct ongoing price action.

- Draw your own overhead resistance and underlying support lines. Just mark 4 S/R levels with 2 above and 2 below the current price action.

- Mark levels created by high volume.

- Use dynamic S/R indicated by 10, 50, 100, and 200 EMAs.

- Use following standard S/R to which price reacts.
	- Pivot points.
	- Daily highs and lows.
	- Fibonacci retracement levels.

- Use 20, 50, 100 and 200 EMA as dynamic S/R lines.
	- If price respects 20 EMA then its a strong trend.
	- If price respects 50 EMA then its a medium trend.
	- If price begins touching 100 EMA then its medium to weak trend.
	- If price respects 200 EMA then its a weak trend.

### Step 2. Observe the price action

Observe price action as it approaches the [[Area of Value]].

#### Wait for the signal (RSI Divergence)

While the price is still trending wait for the RSI divergences to occur.

A divergence is a sign of diminishing momentum. A trend which is strong initially would eventually slow down before consolidating or reversing. Divergence helps in spotting this slowing down of trend before time.

- If there are no divergences ignore the RSI OB/OS levels.
- If you spot a bearish divergence then plan a short trade when price crosses below 80 (The RSI Overbought Zone).
- If you spot a bullish divergence then plan a long trade when price crosses above 20 (The RSI Oversold Zone).

### Step 3. Wait for the price action to approach [[Area of Value]]

Upon spotting a divergence, wait for the price to touch market structure (nearby S/R, trendline etc).

In view of the losing momentum indicated by the divergences the price will most likely react.

When the price touches the nearby S/R level, again check the new divergence status. Is it still there or due to new price move the divergence is gone?

#### Wait for OB/OS crossover

If the divergence is still valid while price is at the S/R level, then you can enter a trade as soon as RSI crosses the OB/OS level.

#### Challenges

- There are not as many bullish divergences as there are bearish divergences.

### Step 4. Extrapolate a high probability move

Look at the recent history to know what past RSI divergences told you about the current price action right now.

| Divergence | Uptrend     | Downtrend   |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Bullish    | HL X LL (H) | LL X HL     |
| Bearish    | HH X LH     | LH X HH (H) |

Predict an upward price move if one or more of the following holds true:
- The downtrend price swings w/ RSI indicates a regular bullish divergence.
- The uptrend price swings w/ RSI indicates a hidden bullish divergence.
- The uptrend price swings w/ RSI does NOT indicate a regular bearish divergence.
- The downtrend price swings w/ RSI does NOT indicate a hidden bearish divergence.

Predict a downward price move if one or more of the following holds true:
- The uptrend price swings w/ RSI indicates a regular bearish divergence.
- The downtrend price swings w/ RSI indicates a hidden bearish divergence.
- The downtrend price swings w/ RSI does NOT indicate a regular bullish divergence.
- The uptrend price swings w/ RSI does NOT indicate a hidden bullish divergence.

### Step 5. Enter the trade (OB/OS Levels)

- Go long as RSI goes above 20.
- Go short as RSI goes below 80

Put a system stop far away so that you are not taken down by choppy price action. System stop is just to protect you from a catestrophic loss. Exit the trade manually.

### Step 6. Monitor the trade (RSI Divergence)

Monitor divergences in the direction of the price movement.
- For long trade monitor for a regular/hidden bearish divergence.
- For short trade monitor for a regular/hidden bullish divergence.
- Keep an eye on price action around market structure along the way.

### Step 7. Exit the trade (Price action and OB/OS Levels)

- Close short position by going long after RSI goes above 20.
- Close long position by going short after RSI goes below 80.
- Close position after spotting a divergence against the direction of the trade.
- Close position if price reacts on the market structure and moves against you.
