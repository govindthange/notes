# Trade rules, not impulses

__Trade your set of rules, not your impulses.__

- Inability to maintain discipline and self-control is the most common psychological mistake.
- Most often than not, impulive trades turn out to be losers.

## What leads to impulsive trading?

- `Not having a plan` is the biggest factor which leads to impulsive trading.
- Instead of seeing the potential risk of not following your rules, you only see opportunities passing you by. That is a dangerous way of trading.

## How to fix impulsive trading?

- The solution is to `plan your trade` so you can `trade your plan`.
- Know the potential risk of not following your rules/plan.
- By planning your trade in advance, you are setting the ground rules, as well as your limits.

## How to stand aside from the fear/greed cycles?

Buy fear and sell greed. And to do so you must stay level headed!

- Know what you are looking for.
- Know how you plan to act if the market does what you anticipate.

	- [[#Lower the risk]]

### Handle fear

Once you are in fear, zoom out the charts. Analyze weekly and monthly charts.

If you are overwhelmed by fear or euphoria stay away from trading.

- Do not enter trade due to the fear of missing out (FOMO). There are ample opportunities and you must be patient to wait.
- Do not enter trade out of euphoria. You must have an entry pre planned.
- Do not exit trade due to the fear of losing out on profits or cutting losses. You must have an exit plan before entering the trade and you must follow it without yielding to fear.

### Handle greed

If you feel too greedy you will lose opportunities. Learn to leave some profits on the table.

- Do not time your entries to catch the market tops & bottoms.
- Plan to take some portion of profit out along the way. Do not wait for the ultimate level to maximize gains.
- Exit the trade once the target is met. Follow your plan.

# Develop good trading habits

Winning in the market is a function of habits. Unfortunately, so is losing.

- __Making money trading is simply a matter of repeating the same effective steps time after time.__
- Its all about keeping good habits. But the opposite is true is you have bad habits!

## What are bad habits?

- Failing to manage your trading risk.
- Revenge trading after taking a loss.
- Jumping from one system to another.
- Sticking to a bad trading plan.
- Getting emotional and impatient.

## How to remove bad habits?

Even if you cannot control the market, you can control your own behavior.

- Find your bad habits.
- Find your weaknesses.

	- [[#^4cde4c]]
- Replace them with good habits.

## What are good habits?

- Being disciplined.
- Balancing schedule and life style.
- Trading often.
- Trading small.
- Avoiding big risks.
- Keeping a trading journal.
- Constantly learning, adjusting, and adapting.

# Trade often

Trade often...
- to minimize the impact of any single losing trade.
	- Minimize the impact that a losing trade may have on an account.
	- Its much easier to take a loss when the real impact on your account is minimal.
- to build skills.
- to refine strategy.

## What is the reality of trading?

Its hard to implement your knowledge & skills in live market conditions.

## Why its hard to implement your knowledge?

- Every day, every trend, every pullback is different.
- Nothing looks exactly the same as it did in the textbook examples.

## How to overcome it?

To get proficient at implementing a method, you need to practice, a lot.

# Trade small

Keep your trade size small...
- to reduce the risk of ruin.
	- Risk of ruin is the probability that you lose so much money you can no longer continue trading.
	- If you have the capital you are in the trading game, if you don't you are out.
- to reduce emotional involvement.
	- Trading without emotions is very hard, as most traders are emotional and reactionary, and are affected on a daily basis by fear and greed.
	- Lowering the trade size is the easiest ways to decreaes the emotional effect.
	- In this way, even if the trade doesn't go as expected, you get the chance to learn from your mistakes without major consequences.
- to give you the chance to learn from your mistakes without major consequences.

# Lower the risk

Keep your risk low to protect your account.

Even when you are getting good at trading you must keep your risk low as you will still have losing period and when you do you want to be able to absorb them well and not take big chunk out of your account.

## How to protect your account?

- Look at the bigger picture.
- Eyeing the possible profits.
- Looking for ways to trade with a lower risk.

# Compete against yourself

Being a trader is ultimately an act of competing against yourself. It's about self-improvement; about being better than you were the day before.

## What is self-improvement?

- Being consistent over the long-run.
- Being diligent and learning from your mistakes.
- Keeping emotions in check.
- Staying within your risk tolerance.

## How to improve?

- Practice
- Self-review
	- Look at all your trades and emotions for the day.
	- Assess how well you followed your trading plan on each.
	- Find weaknesses.
		- Did you take too many trades that weren't part of your trading plan. ^161861
		- Did you look at the chart all day and identified trades that you were supposed to take but didn't? ^4cde4c

# Keep up the motivation

If when you lose on a trade, you learn from your mistakes, and it motivates you to work even harder, you're on the right track!

## What brings the motivation down?

- You've had a bad trading period.
- You feel that you are not advancing as quickly as you'd like.
- You have a small account.
- No matter what you do it seems like it's not worth all the effort.

## How to solve this problem?

Make a list including your mistakes and lessons learnt and have it in front of your screen while trading.

Having a list with the mistakes made right in front of your eyes every day, reincforces positive habits and ensures that you avoid repeating those mistakes.

# Don't get attached to any single trade

Trading opportunities are endless, there's always another one coming. Don't get attached to any single trade.

Do not become emotionally involved in one single trade.

Trading must not be about a single trade. Train yourself to accept that not every trade can be a winning trade, and that you must accept small losses gracefully and move to the next trade.

Allow opportunities to come to you and never chase a market.

If you miss an entry, so be it. There's another one just around the corner.

# Think long-term

- Don't focus on the individual fluctuations in your account, winners or losers.
- Accept that your account will inevitably have periods with considerable gains and, at other times, significant losses.
- What matters it your long-term results.

Its easy to become excited and overly eager to do more trades when favorable price movements benefit your trading account and there will also be days when the market completely truns against you.

## How to aim long-term?

- Understand that neither extreme will last forever.
- Enduring through the good and bad periods is a skill that enables you to learn and grow.
- One single trade (winner or loser) shouldn't matter.
- Do't make any one trade matter by risking too much money or by feeling desperate to win.
- Pace yourself to win the long-distance trading race.
- Your goal should be to win long-term (aim for consistency).

# Recognize you can't predict

A good trade is someone who recognizes that he cannot predict the markets.

When we have several successful trades in a row, we get that feeling that we invincible, that we know what the market will do next.

Don't make the mistake of thinking you can predict the market.
There are thousands of factors that come into play and we cannot know for certain what will happen tomorrow.

> Too much pride leads to disaster.

Pride in trading can be a competitive emotion. Thinking we can predict the markets can be dangerous. For example:
- Making assumptions that a breakout will occur or that a level will hold off a further move is an attempt to predict the market.
- Adopt `if-then-else model` of thinking and acting.
-` Watch what occurs` around these levels and `then enter as momentum moves` in one direction or the other.
- By doing so we are not predicting something will occur but instead we `enter into the current price flow`.
- This makes trading "matter of fact" as opposed to emotional.

# Create your own system

To get long-term consistency you must create your own system by absorbing what is useful, discarding what isn't, and adding what is uniquely your own.

Have the growth mindset is the key to success.
- Trading means continuing education.

- You need to remain focused on learning each day.

## How to have growth mindset?

- Learn each day.
- Implement what is useful.
- Discard what isn't useful.

-  Implement what's right for you.
