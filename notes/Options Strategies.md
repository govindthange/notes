### 5 Rules

#### Rule 1

Exit from trade as soon you make Rs 4,000/- in profit in Bank Nifty and Rs 3,000/- in profit in Nifty

#### Rule 2

Adjust your position only when one side of the premium falls 50% below the higher trading premium

#### Rule 3

The premium of the adjusted position has to be between 80% to 95% of the higher side premium.

#### Rule 4

Difference between the `Call` and `Put` premium should not be less than 80% during the market closing hours.

When you adjust the position the range can be between 80% to 95% range.

#### Rule 5

Exit the trade when you reach a point where both `Call` and `Put` both are at the same `Strike Price`
